# Viby — User Reporting & Blocking Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Viby gives every user tools to stay safe. This policy explains how reporting and blocking work and what to expect.

## Blocking
Tap **⋯ → Block** on any profile, message, or chat. When you block someone:
- You disappear from each other's Discover, feed, search, groups, and chat.
- Existing conversations are hidden and they can't contact you.
- Blocking is private — they aren't told.
Manage blocks in **Settings → Privacy → Blocked users**.

## Reporting
Tap **⋯ → Report** on a profile, photo, post, message, or group. Choose a reason:
- Harassment or bullying
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Nudity or sexual content
- Threats or violence
- Minor safety concern
- Spam or scam
- Fake account / impersonation
- Other (with details)

You may add a short description. Reports are confidential — the reported user is not told who reported them.

## What happens next
1. **Triage by severity.** Minor-safety and credible-threat reports are escalated **immediately**; others are queued for review (target first response **[24–48h]**).
2. **Review.** Our moderation team (and automated systems) assess against the [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md) and [Content Policy](./content-policy.md).
3. **Action.** Possible outcomes: no action, content removal, warning, feature limits, suspension, or permanent ban. Illegal content is reported to the relevant authorities.
4. **Follow-up.** For serious reports we may notify you of the outcome where appropriate and lawful.

## Emergencies
If you or someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. For urgent safety issues on Viby, email **[safety@viby.app]** with "URGENT".

## Abuse of reporting
Knowingly false or mass-malicious reports may themselves result in action against the reporter.

## Appeals
If you believe an action was a mistake, email **[safety@viby.app]** within **[14] days**.
