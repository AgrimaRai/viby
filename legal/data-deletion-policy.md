# Viby — Account & Data Deletion Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

> Google Play **requires** an in-app and a publicly reachable web path to request account and data deletion. This policy documents both.

## How to delete your account
**In-app:** Settings → Account → **Delete account** → confirm. (Profile → Sign out is *not* deletion.)
**Web / email:** visit **[https://viby.app/delete]** or email **[privacy@viby.app]** from your account email with the subject "Delete my account".

## What gets deleted
On confirmed deletion we permanently remove or irreversibly anonymize:
- Profile (name, bio, photos, interests, prompts, location), uploaded images in Storage.
- Posts, comments, reactions, swipes, matches/connections, group memberships.
- Messages you sent (and, where feasible, your identity in conversations).
- Auth record and session tokens.

## What we may retain (and why)
- Limited records required for **legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or safety** purposes (e.g., records of a banned user to prevent re-registration), kept only as long as necessary.
- Aggregated/anonymized analytics that cannot identify you.
- Backups are purged on a rolling **[30-day]** cycle.

## Timeline
- Deletion is processed within **[30] days** of a confirmed request; most data is removed within **[7] days**, with backups expiring within **[30] days**.
- We'll confirm by email when complete.

## Partial deletion
You can also delete individual photos, posts, or messages in-app without deleting your whole account.

Questions: **[privacy@viby.app]**.
