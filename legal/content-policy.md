# Viby — Content Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy defines what may and may not be posted in profiles, photos, prompts, activity posts, group content, and messages. It supplements the [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md) and [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## Allowed
- Authentic photos of yourself and your lifestyle/hobbies.
- Genuine bios, prompts, activity posts (cafés, gym, runs, books, travel, creative work, daily life).
- Respectful conversation and community discussion.

## Prohibited content
- **Sexual & nudity:** explicit content, nudity, sexual solicitation, fetish content.
- **Minor safety (zero tolerance):** any content sexualizing or endangering minors — removed and reported to authorities (e.g., NCMEC where applicable).
- **Violence & harm:** threats, gore, terrorism, self-harm or suicide promotion, dangerous challenges.
- **Hate & harassment:** slurs, dehumanizing content, targeted harassment, doxxing.
- **Illegal goods/activity:** drugs, weapons, stolen goods, fraud.
- **Spam & deception:** ads, scams, phishing, malware links, fake identities, engagement manipulation.
- **IP infringement:** content you don't have rights to.
- **Personal data:** others' private info without consent.

## Moderation process
1. **Proactive:** uploaded images are screened by automated moderation before becoming public; text is filtered for high-severity terms.
2. **Reactive:** user [reports](./user-reporting-policy.md) are triaged by severity; high-severity (minor safety, credible threats) are escalated immediately.
3. **Actions:** content removal, warnings, feature limits, temporary suspension, or permanent ban. Repeat offenders are removed.
4. **Appeals:** email **[safety@viby.app]** within **[14] days** of an action.

## Notice & takedown
To report illegal content or IP infringement, contact **[abuse@viby.app]** with the content link/ID and a description.
