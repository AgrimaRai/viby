# Viby — Community Guidelines

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Viby is for making real friends and finding your people through shared hobbies and lifestyle. Keep it warm, safe, and authentic. Breaking these guidelines can lead to content removal, warnings, suspension, or a permanent ban.

## Be real
- Be **18+** and yourself. No fake accounts, impersonation, or bots.
- Use your own photos. No misleading or AI-faked identity photos.

## Be kind & safe
- No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination (race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, etc.).
- No stalking, doxxing, or sharing others' private information.
- No unwanted sexual advances. Respect "no" and boundaries.

## Keep it clean
- No nudity, sexual content, or sexual solicitation.
- No graphic violence, gore, self-harm promotion, or dangerous activities.
- No illegal content, drugs/weapons sales, or content sexualizing minors (zero tolerance — reported to authorities).

## No spam or scams
- No advertising, mass solicitation, crypto/MLM schemes, phishing, or off-platform money requests.
- No scraping, automation, or manipulating discovery/rankings.

## Meeting people
- Meet in public, tell a friend, trust your instincts. Viby does not run background checks.
- Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable — see [User Reporting Policy](./user-reporting-policy.md).

## Enforcement
We use reports, automated detection, and human review. Tools available to you: **Report** and **Block** on any profile, post, message, or group. Serious or repeat violations result in removal. We may notify law enforcement where required.

Questions or appeals: **[safety@viby.app]**.
